# Character Creation — The Questions of Lushiris

Before the story begins, the goddess **Lushiris** tests the young hero with a
chain of questions. Your answers are not flavour: they secretly forge
**Diehärte**'s entire growth — which unit type he becomes at each rank, his
starting stats and skills, a few bonus items, and even how close he starts to
each heroine.

> **The protagonist's default name is Diehärte.** If you rename him, every
> "Diehärte" below is simply your chosen name.

There are **two layers** to the quiz:

```
 ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │  LAYER 1 — Attribute path        Q1 ─ Q11                    │
 │     Sets your unit type at Lv 1 / Lv 10 / Lv 30 / Lv 50.     │
 │                                                             │
 │  LAYER 2 — Personality           Q12 ─ Q27                   │
 │     Stat boosts, learned skills, bonus items, heroine        │
 │     affinity. No more branching — just rewards.             │
 └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Stat shorthand: **STR** strength · **DEF** defense · **HP** vitality ·
**INT** intelligence · **CHA** charisma · **ATK** attack-mod · **DMG** damage-mod ·
**MOV** move-mod · **Mercs** mercenary count · **Merc-HP** mercenary HP-regen ·
**MDef** magic resistance. Affinity bonuses raise that heroine's love index.

# Scenario 01 — Assault on the Floating Castle
*The day the sky fortress fell*

> For ages, Larcuss had prospered, set between the Rigüler Empire to the north and allied Barral to the south. Above Larcussia, the Floating Castle kept peace.

On the morning of his investiture, Diehärte Klaus's dream of knighthood is
shattered: Field Marshal Altemüller's black-winged host descends on the Floating
Castle under cover of the storm. This opening battle is a tutorial — and it
cannot be won.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Victory** | "Total destruction of the enemy" — *unattainable by design* |
| **Defeat** | Diehärte falls |
| **Ends when** | Altemüller reaches and destroys the Magic Cannon (the crystal) |
| **New allies** | — (prologue) |

## Order of battle

| Side | Units |
|---|---|
| **Allies (NPC)** | Citizen ×2 (Lv 1) · Sir Gerold (Lv 48) · Marquis William (Lv 27) |
| **Enemy** | **Field Marshal Altemüller (Lv 63)** · General Varna (Lv 32) · Hawk Knight ×3 (Lv 14) · Hawk Knight ×3 (Lv 1) · endless reinforcements |

## Buried treasure

| Where | Reward |
|---|---|
| Top of the stairs to the **left of the crystal** | Magic Herb |
| Behind the **broken pillar**, west side of the map | Magic Herb |

## Walkthrough

The victory line reads "destroy all enemies," but the reinforcements never stop —
the scenario ends only when Altemüller breaks the **Magic Cannon**. You cannot
save the castle, so don't try to hold it. Treat the fight as free experience:
the rank-and-file cannot harm the crystal, so leave it undefended and farm kills,
especially the **Lv 1 Hawk Knights**, before the end.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Throughout     Reinforcements are endless — do not try
                to clear the map or defend the crystal.
 Farming        Hunt the Lv 1 Hawk Knights for easy XP.
 Scenario ends  When Altemüller destroys the Magical
                Cannon, the castle falls.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

## Branching dialogue

As the castle falls, Diehärte chooses his parting words to Tiaris — the first
seed of her love index *(post-battle escape scene)*:

| JP (balizador) | EN (in-game) | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 笑顔を見せて。 | Don't lose hope. | Tiaris −1 |
| ジェリオールが‥‥ | Gerold needs us. | Tiaris +1 |
| おいて行くぞ！ | You'll be left behind! | Tiaris −1 |



# Scenario 02 — Madness in Laffel
*Flight through a burning vice-capital*

> Altemüller's scheme brought the Floating Castle crashing down. The young knight and his party barely escaped, and made their way to the city of Laffel.

With the Floating Castle lost, Diehärte carries the wounded Gerold to the vice
capital Laffel — only to find King Wilder of allied Barral has gone mad and
turned on Larcuss. There is no winning this fight; the goal is to escape south
with Layla and Tiaris alive.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Victory** | Diehärte, Layla and Tiaris reach the **south edge** of the map |
| **Defeat** | Diehärte falls · Layla falls |
| **Turn 6** | King Wilder's host appears top-left and gives chase |
| **New ally** | Silver Wolf (arrives turn 6) |

## Order of battle

| Side | Units |
|---|---|
| **Allies (NPC)** | Citizen ×3 (Lv 1) · Layla (Lv 3) · *turn 6:* Silver Wolf (Lv 20) + bandit (Lv 2) |
| **Enemy** | Dios (Lv 25) · Ruud (Lv 15) · Fighter ×4 (Lv 1) · Lord ×3 (Lv 1) · Knight (Lv 1) |
| **Enemy (turn 6)** | **King Wilder (Lv 55)** · Barral Supreme Commander (Lv 20) · Highlord (Lv 19) · Gladiator (Lv 19) · Silver Knight ×2 (Lv 20) |

## Buried treasure

| Where | Reward |
|---|---|
| Southwest **bare-grass patch** (clearing where the grass is worn away) | Ambrosia |
| **East gate** plateau — climb the stairs, just below the top | Magic Herb |
| Rescue **every villager** | Angel Ring |

## Walkthrough

Don't be fooled by the "defeat all enemies" line — you are outnumbered and
out-leveled. Run **south**. The turn-6 wave led by King Wilder spawns at the
top-left, so by then you should already be near the bottom and able to slip away
cleanly. The starting Lv 1 fighters and lords are free experience if you have a
spare turn or two before the chase begins.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Turns 1–5     Push south. Mop up the Lv 1 rabble for XP
               and shepherd the three civilians along.
 Turn 6        Silver Wolf joins; King Wilder's column
               appears top-left and chases.
 Goal          Get Diehärte, Layla and Tiaris onto the
               southern edge to end the scenario.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

## Branching dialogue

None in this scenario.

## Walkthrough

Grove keeps **resurrecting** his ghouls and ghost until he himself falls, so this
is an excellent place to grind — farm the endless undead for experience, then
close on Grove to end it. Keep your civilians screened; their deaths are a loss
condition.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Turn 1        Engage the undead; protect the civilians.
 Turn 2        Gilbert joins.
 Grind         Grove revives his minions endlessly —
               farm XP as long as you like.
 Finish        Kill Grove to win.
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

The enemy's **flying units** are the real danger — they swing wide to hit your
column from behind, so screen your soft units with spears and bows. General
Geier himself towers above your level; victory only needs the *rest* of his
troop cleared, so never trade lives trying to fell him.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Turn 1        The lone Lv 10 Fighter withdraws.
 Turn 2        You're asked how to handle the NPCs —
               send them toward Diehärte, away from the
               fliers in the open.

```

# Scenario 05 — Brilliant General Emerick
*Reaching Baron Torrand's manor*

> Field Marshal Altemüller, informed by Geier, sent Emerick to Torrand's manor to strike before Diehärte's party arrived.

Geier's tip sends the resourceful **General Emerick** to intercept Diehärte at
Baron Torrand's estate. The baron's daughter **Luna** fights at your side — and

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Victory** | Defeat **Emerick** · clear the intercept force · all allies reach the castle |
| **Defeat** | Diehärte falls · Baron Torrand falls |
| **Allies** | Luna, Baron Torrand and his men |

## Order of battle

| Side | Units |
|---|---|
| **Allies (NPC)** | Luna (Lv 10) · Baron Torrand (Lv 20) · Highlord ×2 (Lv 14) · Archer ×2 (Lv 10) · Citizen ×2 (Lv 5) |
| **Enemy** | **General Emerick (Lv 28)** · Lord ×4 (Lv 12) · Knight ×4 (Lv 13) · Fighter ×4 (Lv 11) · Gladiator ×2 (Lv 20) |

## Buried treasure

| Where | What to do | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Clearing **ringed by 3 trees**, beside your spawn | Pick "Strike a pose" (決めポーズをとる) | Laurin's Ring |
| **Southeast** — the one frame whose carved face is **upside-down** | Pick "It intrigues me" (気になるからだ) | Devil Axe |
| **Northeast**, the water-gate switch | Stand a unit on it | Holy Talisman |

## Walkthrough

Emerick holds off attacking Torrand until every civilian has escaped, which buys
you time — but **Luna charges straight for the castle** and will be swarmed if you
don't keep pace. The **gladiators** flanking the castle hit hard enough to grind
your highlords to pulp, so deal with them deliberately. Clearing fast also feeds
Luna's love index.

```
 BATTLE FLOW
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Opening       Escort the civilians out; Emerick waits.
 Keep pace     Follow Luna toward the castle so she is
```

# Endings & the Best-Ending Route

Langrisser III has **two endings** — a *normal* ending and the *true* (best)
ending — each with **variations by which heroine** you win. This chapter pulls the
requirements together; for the per-choice love-index detail, see *The Heroine
System*.

## The two endings

| Ending | How it happens |
|---|---|
| **Normal** | The game ends after **Scenario 34**. |
| **True (best)** | Meeting the checklist in Scenario 34 opens **Scenarios 35 & 36**, the true finale. |

> On the true route your **chosen lover is kidnapped** and cannot be used in
> Scenarios 35–36 (story reasons); **Altemüller and Varna** join in her place.

## Reaching the true ending

Everything below must come together at **Scenario 34** (the crossroads):

**Prerequisites carried into Sc 34**
1. A **successful love confession** (offered after Scenario 33).
2. **Freya** persuaded and in your party (her arc closes in Scenario 31).
3. **Langrisser equipped** on Diehärte.

**Inside Scenario 34**
4. **Persuade Varna** with Emerick or Diehärte (move adjacent — automatic); **keep her alive**.
5. **Persuade Altemüller** with **Emerick** (move adjacent — automatic); **keep Emerick alive**.
6. **Defeat Altemüller with Diehärte wielding Langrisser.**

Do all six and Scenarios 35 & 36 open.

## Winning Freya (the extra requirement)

Freya joins **only** if you take every **persuasion** option across her arc — and
avoid attacking her or wiping out the field when she's present. The persuasion
beats, in order:

> Throughout: **never have Diehärte attack Freya, and don't defeat the Dark Knight**
> in Sc 6/16 — each lowers her index. (See Sc 32/33 for a few more ± choices.)

## How your characters are remembered

Whatever ending you reach, the epilogue narrates **what becomes of each
character** afterward — one rises to a great knight, another to a famed mage,
and so on. Each fate is decided by that character's record at clear time:
**final level**, **total enemies defeated**, and **total retreats**. Roughly:

| Their record at clear | Their fate |
|---|---|
| Many kills · **zero retreats** | Great deeds — their name lives on in history |
| Few kills · **zero retreats** | A quiet, peaceful life ever after |
| Any kills · **one or more retreats** | Dies, or vanishes without a trace |
| Any kills · **many retreats** | Comes to a wretched end |

So if a character means something to you, **fight hard with them and never let
them retreat** — that is how you earn them a happy life after the war.

## After the credits

Once the ending and staff roll finish, **leave the screen alone** — wait without
pressing anything and the cast's **voice-actor free-talk** begins. Press a button
and you're bounced back to the title, so be patient. (See also the **Hero Select**
gag and **New Game+** in *Secrets & Cheats*.)

# Secrets & Cheats

A grab-bag of codes and tricks. Buttons are Sega Saturn controller faces
(A B C · X Y Z · L R · Start) and the D-pad. Items marked *(reported)* are
documented but unconfirmed.

## Codes

| Cheat | How |
|---|---|
| **Hidden shop** | In battle prep, open **Shop** → at the buy/sell screen press **Y, A, ↓, X, B**. A chime confirms it; **Buy** now lists *every* item (if you can afford them). |
| **Scenario / level select** | At the prep screen choose **Load**, pick a scenario save, then press **→, X, ←, Y, A** (chime). Use **←/→** to choose the scenario. Enables New Game+ and the secret-stage replays. |
| **Sound test** | At the **title screen**, press **Z, A, Y, ←, X, →, C** — plays BGM, SE, and even each character's scenario voice clips. |
| **MP bug** | Equip a **Tiara** on a magic user, spend down to **MP ≤ 5**, then **unequip** the Tiara → MP jumps to **99 and never drains**. |
| **More experience** *(reported)* | During battle, open a unit's move menu and press **Z, Start, ↑, R, X, ↓, B + Start**. |
| **Money blast** *(reported)* | In prep, **Shop** → buy/sell screen → highlight **Sell**, press **Y, A, ↓, X, B + Start**. |

## Tricks & Easter eggs

- **Infinite P glitch.** At the **Sell** screen with **2+ pages** of items, put the
  cursor at the top of page 2 and keep selling even after that page empties (don't
  flip pages). A **"stick" (棒)** item appears above the cursor — sell it over and
  over for endless P. *Don't move the cursor up, or it jams.* Unequip **Langrisser**
  and have the whole party hold nothing before trying; if a sale price drops below
  1000 P, save, soft-reset, and retry.
- **Hero Select.** Obtain the **Iron Dumbbell** and **Vaseline** (no need to equip
  them), then reach the **confession scene** (after Scenario 33) — and enjoy the
  surprise.
- **New Game+.** With **scenario-select**, replay with your grown roster and items
  carried over. The game has **second- and third-playthrough dialogue and
  branches** — e.g. defeating Altemüller back in Scenario 1, or replaying a
  Langrisser-acquisition map while already holding the sword. Discover them
  yourself.
- **OP movie, quad-split.** At boot, after the Masaya logo fades to black and
  before the opening starts, press **2P Start four times** — the OP then plays as a
  2×2 split, which oddly looks crisper.
- **CD-ROM bonus.** Pop the game disc into a **PC** CD-ROM drive to find graphics
  data and messages from the developers.

## After the credits

Let the ending and staff roll finish, then **leave the screen idle** — wait and the
**voice actors' free-talk** begins. Any button press returns you to the title, so
sit tight.
