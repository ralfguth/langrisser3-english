Character Creation — The Questions of Lushiris

Before the story begins, the goddess Lushiris tests the young hero with a chain of questions. Your answers are not flavour: they secretly forge Diehärte's entire growth — which unit type he becomes at each rank, his starting stats and skills, a few bonus items, and even how close he starts to each heroine.

The protagonist's default name is Diehärte. If you rename him, every "Diehärte" below is simply your chosen name.

There are two layers to the quiz:

 ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │  LAYER 1 — Attribute path        Q1 ─ Q11                    │
 │     Sets your unit type at Lv 1 / Lv 10 / Lv 30 / Lv 50.     │
 │                                                             │
 │  LAYER 2 — Personality           Q12 ─ Q27                   │
 │     Stat boosts, learned skills, bonus items, heroine        │
 │     affinity. No more branching — just rewards.             │
 └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Stat shorthand: STR strength · DEF defense · HP vitality · INT intelligence · CHA charisma · ATK attack-mod · DMG damage-mod · MOV move-mod · Mercs mercenary count · Merc-HP mercenary HP-regen · MDef magic resistance. Affinity bonuses raise that heroine's love index.

Scenario 01 — Assault on the Floating Castle

The day the sky fortress fell

For ages, Larcuss had prospered, set between the Rigüler Empire to the north and allied Barral to the south. Above Larcussia, the Floating Castle kept peace.

On the morning of his investiture, Diehärte Klaus's dream of knighthood is shattered: Field Marshal Altemüller's black-winged host descends on the Floating Castle under cover of the storm. This opening battle is a tutorial — and it cannot be won.

Walkthrough

The victory line reads "destroy all enemies," but the reinforcements never stop — the scenario ends only when Altemüller breaks the Magic Cannon. You cannot save the castle, so don't try to hold it. Treat the fight as free experience: the rank-and-file cannot harm the crystal, so leave it undefended and farm kills, especially the Lv 1 Hawk Knights, before the end.

How your characters are remembered

Whatever ending you reach, the epilogue narrates what becomes of each character afterward — one rises to a great knight, another to a famed mage, and so on. Each fate is decided by that character's record at clear time: final level, total enemies defeated, and total retreats. Roughly:

Their record at clearTheir fate
Many kills · zero retreatsGreat deeds — their name lives on in history
Few kills · zero retreatsA quiet, peaceful life ever after
Any kills · one or more retreatsDies, or vanishes without a trace
Any kills · many retreatsComes to a wretched end

So if a character means something to you, fight hard with them and never let them retreat — that is how you earn them a happy life after the war.

Classes & Class Change

Every commander can change class as they level. Each character belongs to an attribute line (Infantry, Cavalry, Mage…); leveling within a class — or using a Divine Blessing — promotes them up that line. Reach the top and the right item can open a hidden class, which usually grants powerful new skills.

The hero and Holy Beast Jugler are special: various Runes add attributes to them regardless of level (see the Items chapter).
